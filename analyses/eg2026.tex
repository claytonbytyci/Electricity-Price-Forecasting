\section*{Abstract}
%----------------------------------------------------------------------
\textit{%
We construct hourly day-ahead forecasts for the 1st week of 2024 and an average hourly day-ahead forecast for all the weeks of 2024 using parametric methods and a LSTM neural network. We find that an ensemble over our models, which exploits their respective strengths, proves to be superior in generating reliable forecasts.
}
